Privacy

WebNovel Dark & Enhanced · last updated 2026-11-03

This extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. There is no account, analytics, telemetry, advertising or remote code.

What is stored

  • Theme, cleanup, listing and reader preferences.
  • Your filter lists (hidden/preferred tags, blocked books and authors, keywords).
  • Cached author catalogues (public book ids) and per-book reading positions.
  • The last update-check result.

All of it lives in chrome.storage.local on your device. Use Export settings in the options page for a backup; Reset removes it.

Network requests

  • /book/<id>/catalog on webnovel.com, to build the table of contents and chapter navigation.
  • /profile/<id> on webnovel.com when you block an author, to import their public book list.
  • The latest release tag from api.github.com (fallback gitlab.com/api) every 12 hours, or when you press Check for updates. Only the release tag and URL are read; no data is sent.

Permissions

storage keeps the data above; alarms schedules the update and author refreshes; the host permissions are limited to webnovel.com and the two release feeds.
